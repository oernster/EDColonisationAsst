Features

Everything EDCA tracks comes from your journal as you fly. The short version is here; colonisation, fleet carriers and getting it running each have a page of their own.

What it is

📓

Journal-driven, automatic

EDCA reads your Elite Dangerous journal as you play and pushes what changed straight to the page. Deliver a load and the numbers move while you are still at the pad, with nothing to refresh and nothing to type in. Even your commander name comes from the journal rather than a settings box.

📱

Browser HUD, tablet-ready

A lightweight web HUD you can open on a second screen or a cockpit tablet over your own network, with a keep-screen-awake option for long sessions.

🔒

Local-first, no accounts

EDCA runs entirely on your machine. No external services, no accounts and no telemetry; it reads your journal and nothing of yours leaves your PC. The one thing it ever sends anywhere is a version check when it starts: an anonymous request asking GitHub what the latest release is, carrying no identifier and no journal data. It prompts you when a new version is out, so you hear about upgrades without being watched.
