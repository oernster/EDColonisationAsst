EDCA

Elite Dangerous Colonisation Assistant

Colonisation runs on one question: what still needs hauling and where to. EDCA answers it from your journal as you fly, so the shopping list, your carrier's hold and where that carrier is heading are all current without you tabbing out. No spreadsheets, no accounts, nothing leaves your PC.

What it is

📓

Journal-driven, automatic

EDCA reads your Elite Dangerous journal as you play and pushes what changed straight to the page. Deliver a load and the numbers move while you are still at the pad, with nothing to refresh and nothing to type in. Even your commander name comes from the journal rather than a settings box.

📱

Browser HUD, tablet-ready

A lightweight web HUD you can open on a second screen or a cockpit tablet over your own network, with a keep-screen-awake option for long sessions.

🔒

Local-first, no accounts

EDCA runs entirely on your machine. No external services, no accounts and no telemetry; it reads your journal and nothing of yours leaves your PC. The one outbound call is a version check your browser makes against the GitHub releases page, so you hear about upgrades without being watched.
